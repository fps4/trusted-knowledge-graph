Architecture & decisions

One resolver in front of a graph and an index

The graph is anchored to the firm's systems of record, not scraped out of text. Documents stay in their store and their index. One resolver decides what a given person may see — before any evidence is assembled — and writes down what it decided.

The path of one question

PlanPick the competency-question template; validate slots.
TermsResolve words in the glossary; an ambiguous one is refused with its readings.
RouteGraph, index, hybrid or refuse — with the reason.
BindFill the template with validated values.
DecideCandidates → lineage → OPA → bind the query to what was allowed.
RetrieveGraph query; passages only under a permit, filtered inside the index query.
Compose or refuseAnswer with sources and withheld count, or refuse with grounds. Record either way.

Decisions, in short

Each is an architecture decision record in the repository — context, options, consequences.

Data and the graph

ADRDecisionWhy
0001 The spine is mapped from systems of record with R2RMLWho led what, for whom, when, is already known — extracting it from text is weaker
0006 No document content in the graph — a shape fails any literal over 500 charactersThe graph holds facts and links; text stays where its access is enforced
0014 A fact is a node with confidence, review state, who asserted it and whenA lawyer acts on a fact only when they can see how sure it is and where it came from
0016 SALI LMSS by mapping, not adoptionThe firm's practice areas are not SALI's; mapping keeps both honest
0023 Extraction by Claude through the Batches API, predicates as an enumStructured output that can only say what the ontology allows; half price off the request path

Access

ADRDecisionWhy
0007 The rules live in one file, owned by Risk; barriers and need-to-know are both supportedOne source of truth, compiled to OPA and to the document store
0008 Spine facts decided by their matter; derived facts by lineageA summary or extracted fact inherits the restrictions of everything it came from
0009 Decide, then retrieveFiltering after retrieval means the evidence was already assembled; deciding first means it never is
0010 Whether to say “something was withheld” is a policy settingIt is Risk's call, not the engineer's
0021 The document store enforces its own access, with per-person credentialsA stolen document id yields nothing; no second door around the resolver
0028 A document inherits the matters it citesA note filed on an open matter that cites a walled one must be walled too
0011 Identity is asserted by a per-person key, not federatedThe lab's one known weakness against the design, which uses on-behalf-of tokens
